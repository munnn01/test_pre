## Mechanism and locked search

Measure all six existing candidates at every QP. ResNet18 IMAGENET1K_V1 is frozen/eval with no classifier logits or labels. Source/decoded RGB [0,1] are bilinearly resized to the AR 112x112 grid with align_corners=False and normalized by ImageNet mean/std. This deliberately differs from the standard 256-resize/224-crop recipe; no claim of standard ImageNet accuracy is made. Extract dense layer2 and layer3 maps on all 16 frames, normalize feature channels at every location, and average half squared Euclidean distance between normalized source/decoded features over frames/positions/layers (range [0,2]). Start from frozen V6 v. On allowed QPs only, admit candidates c with relative proxy improvement (D(v)-D(c))/max(D(v),1e-12) > tau_relative, R(c) <= (1+rate_slack)*R(v), and R(c) <= R(identity). Choose smallest (D(c),R(c),fixed candidate order); no eligible candidate retains V6. Source features are computed once per video. No r2plus1d/r3d/mc3 analyzer is constructed in workers. ## Literature basis and limits

The semantic direction tests a proxy hypothesis inspired by [deep perceptual features, Zhang et al., CVPR 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.03924) and [feature-based machine-compression distortion, Fischer et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.08168). These papers do not establish that this selector improves MC3 action recognition. ResNet weight/normalization references: [official torchvision documentation](https://docs.pytorch.org/vision/stable/models/generated/torchvision.models.resnet18.html). The spatial direction isolates the effect of identity rescue at low QPs using a cheap pixel proxy. Both are empirical screening hypotheses; success is CHƯA ĐO.
